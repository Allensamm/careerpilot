Frequently asked questions

Virtual assistance, clearly explained.

Can I choose only the tasks I need help with?

Yes. We agree a defined scope based on your priorities. You can begin with a focused set of tasks and review the arrangement as needs evolve.

Do you provide ongoing support or one-off projects?

Depending on capacity and requirements, support can be scoped as a defined project or an ongoing arrangement. We confirm availability and terms before work starts.

Will you send emails or make decisions on my behalf?

Only within the authority and approval boundaries agreed with you. Sensitive, financial or consequential actions remain subject to your explicit approval.

How do you handle confidential information?

We agree what information is needed, use appropriate access controls and avoid requesting credentials or data that are not necessary. Specific security and confidentiality arrangements are discussed during onboarding.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on scope, complexity, volume and whether support is project-based or ongoing. Contact us with your requirements for a tailored discussion.
