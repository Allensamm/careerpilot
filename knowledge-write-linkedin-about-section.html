How to Write a LinkedIn About Section That Communicates Your Value

The About section explains the story behind your experience. It should answer what you do, what you are good at, what evidence supports that, and what work or professional connection you are open to.

CareerPilot Knowledge Centre · Practical guidance

Understand the topic

Open with a clear statement of your professional focus. Follow with two or three areas of expertise and explain the types of challenges you have worked on.

What to consider

Use evidence: projects delivered, processes improved, customers supported or teams coordinated. Describe context accurately without disclosing confidential information.

Build a practical approach

Write naturally and use short paragraphs. Include relevant keywords in context, not as a disconnected list. Close with a simple invitation to connect.

Important considerations

Draft four short paragraphs: your professional identity, strengths, one or two examples of impact, and your next direction.
