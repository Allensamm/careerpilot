Frequently asked questions

AI productivity, clearly explained.

Do you build custom AI software?

This service focuses on practical workflow design, tool configuration guidance, prompts and process improvement. Bespoke software development is not implied and would need a separate scope and technical assessment.

Which AI tools do you work with?

Tool recommendations depend on your existing systems, requirements, budget and data policies. We discuss suitable options rather than assuming one tool fits every business.

Will AI replace human review?

No. Outputs should be checked, especially when they affect clients, money, employment, legal matters or important business decisions.

Can confidential information be used?

We first review whether the task requires sensitive information and what the selected tool's data terms allow. Do not share confidential or personal data with an AI tool unless you are authorised and the safeguards are appropriate.

How do you measure productivity improvements?

Where practical, we establish a baseline such as time per task, rework or turnaround, then compare after the workflow has been tested. Results depend on usage and context; we do not promise fixed savings.
