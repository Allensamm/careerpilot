15 Administrative Tasks Entrepreneurs Should Stop Doing Themselves

Discover 15 recurring administrative tasks founders can document, delegate and review to protect time for higher-value work.

CareerPilot Knowledge Centre · Practical guidance

Understand the opportunity

Entrepreneurs often become the default owner of every task: booking meetings, chasing replies, formatting documents and updating spreadsheets. That can quietly consume the time needed for sales, strategy and customer relationships.

What to consider

Tasks to consider delegating include inbox sorting, appointment scheduling, meeting agendas, travel comparisons, expense collation, document formatting, contact research, CRM hygiene, invoice reminders, file organisation, routine customer updates, proposal formatting, data entry, event coordination and weekly status reports.

Build a practical approach

For each task, write a short procedure: trigger, steps, tools, deadline, quality standard and escalation point. Share only the access needed. Review early outputs, then move to lighter quality checks once the process is stable.

Put it into practice

Keep pricing decisions, sensitive employee matters, legal approvals and important client commitments with the person who has the authority and context. A VA can prepare information and coordinate a process without taking over reserved decisions.

Your next step

Choose one recurring task this week. Record how you do it, what a good result looks like and what should happen when something unusual occurs. Delegate a small trial before transferring a whole workflow.
