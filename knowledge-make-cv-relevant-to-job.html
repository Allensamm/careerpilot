How to Make Your CV More Relevant to the Job You Want

A relevant CV helps a recruiter see the connection between your experience and the role. Tailoring means selecting the most relevant evidence for each opportunity.

CareerPilot Knowledge Centre · Practical guidance

Understand the topic

Read the job description and identify core responsibilities, essential skills, tools and experience level. Compare them with your actual background and note direct or transferable evidence.

What to consider

Adjust your profile to reflect the role. Reorder skills so the most relevant appear first and give more space to experience that demonstrates the required capabilities.

Build a practical approach

Use employer terminology when it accurately describes your experience. Describe outcomes and use numbers only when you can verify them.

Important considerations

Choose one vacancy. Highlight its five most important requirements, then mark the CV evidence that supports each one. Make the match visible near the top.
