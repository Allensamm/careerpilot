Privacy and information

Privacy notice

A plain-language overview of information handled when you use the CareerPilot website.

Information you choose to provide

When you contact CareerPilot or prepare an enquiry, you may provide your name, email address, service interests, project context and preferred timing. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers or other sensitive information through general website forms.

Browser-based tools

Some tools on this website prepare summaries, checklists and draft documents in your browser. The project brief builder, service quiz, resource downloads and onboarding preparation features are designed to run locally. They do not transmit the entered content to CareerPilot merely by using the tool. You choose whether to copy, download or send the result.

Enquiries and communications

Where an email application is opened or text is copied, you control whether and when to send it. A form submission or prepared summary does not itself create a client relationship or confirm a booking. The website's contact and discovery request flows may require you to send the prepared details separately.

Cookies, analytics and third parties

CareerPilot uses optional first-party website analytics only after you select "Accept analytics" in the website notice. If you decline, analytics events are not sent. If accepted, the site stores a random browser identifier and session identifier in your browser and records page views, approximate active time, clicks and tool interactions, referring website domain, and general device, browser and operating-system categories. Analytics events do not include names, email addresses, form field values, CV text, uploaded documents or typed planner content. CareerPilot does not intentionally store raw IP addresses; a keyed hash of the request IP is used only to limit repeated attempts to access the private owner dashboard. Analytics records are held in a hosted database. The owner dashboard displays the most recent 90 days; older records remain in the database until removed using the documented retention process. You can withdraw consent by clearing the cp_analytics_consent and cp_analytics_visitor browser storage for this site. Third-party providers may receive technical information when delivering fonts, libraries or database hosting; consult their notices for details.

Professional assessment: CV and profile handling

CareerPilot's Professional Assessment is a one-time service. When the secure assessment workflow is enabled, we collect your full name, email address, CV (PDF or DOCX), optional LinkedIn profile URL, professional biography, career goals, and dated consent and terms acceptance. These materials are used to prepare the assessment you request. We do not publish customer CVs or professional profiles without permission. The platform is operated internally by authorised CareerPilot staff; purchasing the service does not give direct access to the software.

Private submission and access: Uploaded CVs are intended to be kept in a private, non-public storage bucket with access controlled through server-side credentials. Only approved staff authenticated through the restricted administrative service can retrieve them. Never send CVs through public file-sharing links. Uploads are disabled until private storage, a malware scanning integration and the payment service are configured and verified.

Third-party processors: The planned assessment workflow uses Supabase for private storage and order records, Stripe for hosted card payments and Resend for transactional email. A separately configured file-scanning provider must inspect CVs before acceptance; its identity, location and handling terms must be reviewed and disclosed before launch. This implementation does not automatically send CV contents to external generative AI providers. Any such future processing would require a separate disclosed purpose, appropriate safeguards and explicit permission where required.

Defined retention: For an enabled workflow, unpaid uploaded documents are scheduled for deletion 48 hours after the request; unpaid profile details are scheduled for removal after 7 days. Paid CVs are scheduled for deletion 30 days after delivery or 90 days after payment, whichever comes first. Paid profile text and direct identifiers are scheduled for removal 180 days after payment unless earlier deletion is requested. Order identifiers and limited payment metadata may be retained separately where required for accounting, disputes or compliance. A scheduled maintenance job performs automated deletion, with staff-assisted removal requests available. We cannot promise immediate erasure of third-party payment records or provider backups outside our control.

Deletion and requests: You can request deletion of your submitted profile and documents via bazalel@bazaleldesign.com. We will verify the request before removing the information held by the assessment service, subject to applicable obligations. If a provider is not yet configured or the final privacy information is incomplete, the site will not accept private documents or payments.

Retention and security

Do not assume that information entered into a browser-only tool is saved by CareerPilot. Information you independently email or otherwise share may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide services, meet legal obligations or resolve disputes. Appropriate access and security measures should be applied to any client information received.

Your choices and questions

You can choose not to provide optional information and can contact CareerPilot to ask about information you have shared. A dedicated agency privacy contact and jurisdiction-specific details should be confirmed before this notice is treated as a final legal policy.

Important: This is a website privacy notice draft, not legal advice. Before launch, CareerPilot should confirm its legal entity, operating jurisdiction, contact address, data controller details, retention periods, processors, lawful bases and applicable data-subject rights with a qualified adviser.
