LinkedIn & professional branding

How to Optimise Your LinkedIn Profile to Attract Recruiters

A strong LinkedIn profile helps people understand what you do, the problems you solve and the experience you bring. Optimisation is not about repeating keywords; it is about presenting a coherent professional story.

CareerPilot Knowledge Centre · Practical guidance

Understand the topic

A strong LinkedIn profile helps people understand what you do, the problems you solve and the experience you bring. Optimisation is not about repeating keywords; it is about presenting a coherent professional story.

What to consider

Begin with the role you want next. Review genuine job descriptions and identify recurring skills, tools, sector terms and responsibilities. Use only keywords that accurately reflect your experience.

Build a practical approach

Write a headline that combines your professional identity, specialism and value. Use the About section to explain strengths, evidence and direction. In Experience, describe scope and outcomes rather than copying a job description.

Important considerations

Add relevant skills, qualifications and projects. Use Featured for work samples or portfolio links you are comfortable making public. Check dates, spelling, links and privacy settings.

Put it into practice

Choose one target role and compare its requirements with your headline, About section and most recent experience entry. Update sections that are unclear or missing evidence.
