How to Identify Legitimate Remote Job Opportunities

Remote work offers flexibility, but fake vacancies and impersonation scams can target job seekers. A legitimate-looking advert is not proof that an employer or recruiter is genuine.

CareerPilot Knowledge Centre · Practical guidance

Understand the topic

Verify the employer independently. Find its official website and careers page through a trusted search rather than relying only on a link in a message.

What to consider

Be cautious if someone asks you to pay for an application, training, equipment or guaranteed placement. Never share banking credentials or forward money for a supposed employer.

Build a practical approach

Check the email domain, interview process and consistency of company details. Do not share sensitive information until you have verified the organisation and understand why it is needed.

Important considerations

Confirm the vacancy on an official channel, research the recruiter and pause if the process asks for money or unusual personal data.
