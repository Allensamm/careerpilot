How to Organise Your Job Search and Stop Applying Randomly

A structured job search helps you focus on roles that fit your skills, prepare stronger applications and follow up consistently.

CareerPilot Knowledge Centre · Practical guidance

Understand the topic

Define two or three target job families. For each, list essential skills, preferred industries, location or remote requirements and non-negotiables.

What to consider

Use a tracker with company, role, source, date found, closing date, fit notes, CV version, application status, contact and next action.

Build a practical approach

Build a weekly routine: research suitable openings, shortlist roles, tailor applications, prepare for interviews and review progress.

Important considerations

Choose a target role and create a one-page criteria list. Save five suitable vacancies and identify recurring requirements before editing your CV.
