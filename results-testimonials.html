Trust and transparency

Good work should create meaningful progress.

We believe credibility comes from clear expectations, thoughtful delivery and honest feedback. This page sets out the types of work CareerPilot can document; it does not claim these are completed client case studies.

Our evidence standard

We do not invent testimonials, client logos, performance statistics or endorsements. A client story will be published only when it is based on an actual engagement, the details have been checked and the client has given permission for the agreed information to be shared.

Executive and operational support

Where a real engagement permits, we can explain the initial coordination challenge, agreed administrative support, deliverables and documented changes to the working process.

Illustrative case-study category — not a client result.

LinkedIn profile repositioning

With permission, we can describe the profile sections reviewed, positioning decisions and agreed deliverables, without promising recruiter attention or job outcomes.

Illustrative case-study category — not a client result.

Job-search organisation

A future case study may show how target roles, application tracking and preparation were structured, while avoiding unsupported claims about interviews or offers.

Illustrative case-study category — not a client result.

Workflow and productivity improvement

Where measurable evidence exists, we can document the workflow before and after, how measurements were taken and what limitations apply.

Illustrative case-study category — not a client result.

How we report results

  • Agree the problem and baseline before work starts.
  • Describe the work and deliverables accurately.
  • Distinguish measured outcomes from estimates.
  • Obtain written permission before naming a client or sharing a testimonial.
  • Link to public professional profiles only when relevant and authorised.
