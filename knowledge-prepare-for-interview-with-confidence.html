How to Prepare for an Interview With Confidence

Interview confidence comes from preparation and practice. Understand the role, select relevant examples and communicate your thinking clearly.

Start with the role

Read the job description and identify the responsibilities, skills and experience the employer values most. Separate essential requirements from desirable experience, then prepare truthful examples from your own work.

Research the organisation

Use the organisation's official website, product or service pages and credible recent coverage. Understand its customers, work and sector. Avoid relying on rumours or unverified social posts.

Prepare examples with STAR

Use Situation, Task, Action and Result to organise examples. Explain the context briefly, make your own contribution clear and describe what happened. Use figures only when you can substantiate them.

Practise naturally

Practise likely questions aloud, but do not memorise a script. Prepare a concise introduction, why the role interests you, examples of problem-solving and collaboration, and an honest response to areas where you are still developing.

Prepare questions and practical details

Ask about expectations, team structure, priorities and success measures. Confirm the interview time and time zone, location or video link, and any requested materials. Test your technology in advance.

Your next action

Choose three examples: a challenge you solved, a time you worked with others and a result you helped deliver. Practise explaining each in about two minutes, then prepare two questions specific to the employer.
