The LinkedIn Profile Checklist for Professionals in 2026

Review your LinkedIn headline, About section, experience, skills, Featured content and contact details with this practical checklist.

CareerPilot Knowledge Centre · Practical guidance

Understand the topic

Headline: does it describe your professional focus? About: does it explain strengths and direction? Experience: do entries describe responsibilities and outcomes? Skills: are relevant skills easy to find?

What to consider

Check that your profile image and banner are current, Featured links work, education details are accurate and contact details are appropriate.

Build a practical approach

Does LinkedIn match your CV? Have you used target-role language naturally? Have you checked what is publicly visible? Review the profile on desktop and mobile.

Important considerations

Ask a trusted person to describe what you do after a 20-second scan. Clarify your headline and opening lines if their answer differs from your intended positioning.
