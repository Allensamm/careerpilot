Frequently asked questions

A few useful answers before we begin.

Here are answers to common questions about our services and how an engagement works. If you need more detail, get in touch and tell us what you have in mind.

Who does CareerPilot work with?

We support entrepreneurs, founders, executives and busy professionals who need operational assistance, as well as job seekers and professionals who want career and LinkedIn support.

Can I use just one service?

Yes. You can enquire about a focused project or a specific service. Combined support can also be discussed where the scope makes sense.

Do you offer ongoing virtual assistance?

Ongoing support can be discussed based on your needs, expected workload, availability and agreed scope.

Will you apply for jobs on my behalf?

We can support research, preparation and organisation. Applications should be reviewed and approved by you before submission, and we do not misrepresent your experience.

Can you guarantee a job or interview?

No. Hiring decisions are made by employers and outcomes depend on many factors. We focus on the quality and organisation of the support we provide.

How do you use AI?

AI may assist suitable research, drafting or workflow tasks. Human review remains important, especially for accuracy, privacy, judgement and professional reputation.

How do we get started?

Use the contact form to explain what you need. We will review the enquiry and discuss fit, scope, timing and next steps.

How much do services cost?

Pricing depends on the service, complexity, volume and whether support is project-based or ongoing. Contact us for a discussion of your requirements.
