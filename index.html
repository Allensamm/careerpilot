05 / Questions, answered

Know what to expect.

Our service is designed to feel straightforward, considered and personal. Here's how it works.

Am I buying access to an AI platform?

No. CareerPilot is a professional service. Our proprietary AI-powered platform is used internally to help analyse information and prepare recommendations; clients don't purchase direct access to the software.

Can you help improve my CV and LinkedIn profile?

Yes. CareerPilot focuses on how professional experience, skills and achievements are presented in career documents and online profiles. We'll agree on the scope of the review or refinement before work begins.

Will you add achievements or skills to make my profile stronger?

We won't invent qualifications, responsibilities, numbers or achievements. We work with details you provide and help you express them more clearly and effectively.

Do you guarantee interviews or job offers?

No career service can guarantee a hiring decision. We focus on improving the clarity, quality and relevance of your professional presentation; employers make their own decisions.

How do I get started?

Tell us about your career direction through our enquiry page. We'll discuss your goals, confirm which services fit and explain the next steps before requesting relevant career documents.
