09 / Questions answered

Clarity before you commit.

Understand what you're buying, how our technology is used and what happens next.

Do I get access to the CareerPilot AI platform?

No. The technology is proprietary and used internally by the CareerPilot team. You purchase a personalised professional assessment, not a software account.

What does the $50 assessment include?

CV assessment, LinkedIn recommendations, professional bio review, career positioning report and personalised recommendations, subject to the agreed service scope.

Can I upload my CV and pay now?

Not yet through this landing page. Secure document submission and hosted payment must be configured and verified before launch. You can begin by submitting a non-sensitive enquiry through Get Started.

Will CareerPilot apply for jobs or contact recruiters for me?

No. This service is focused on improving how your professional experience is presented. It does not automatically apply for jobs or contact recruiters.

Are interviews or job offers guaranteed?

No. Hiring decisions depend on employers and many factors outside CareerPilot's control. We focus on the quality and clarity of your professional presentation.

How is my personal information handled?

Review the current privacy notice. Secure document intake will not be enabled until access, retention, deletion and any third-party processing arrangements have been defined.
