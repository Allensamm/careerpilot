09 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Straight answers. No guesswork.

Understanding the service is as important as understanding what you'll receive.

Am I buying access to CareerPilot's software?

No. CareerPilot uses its proprietary AI-powered platform internally. You purchase a professional career optimisation service and receive your assessment results, not software credentials.

What is included in the $50 assessment?

A CV assessment, LinkedIn recommendations, professional bio review, career positioning report and personalised improvement guidance, based on the materials you provide.

Does CareerPilot submit job applications or contact recruiters?

No. The professional assessment does not apply for jobs, contact recruiters or make hiring decisions. It focuses on the accuracy, clarity and relevance of your professional presentation.

Is an interview or job offer guaranteed?

No. CareerPilot cannot guarantee interview invitations, recruiter responses, employment or any hiring outcome. The service focuses on actionable career presentation improvements.

How do I provide my CV and LinkedIn profile?

Start with the secure assessment request page. When private document storage and payment are configured, you can submit your materials and complete a hosted checkout. Otherwise, contact CareerPilot before sharing sensitive files.

How is my information handled?

Your documents are intended only for your assessment, with access restricted to authorised staff. Review the privacy notice for current storage, retention, deletion and third-party processing information before submitting.
