Frequently asked questions

LinkedIn optimisation, clearly explained.

Do you log in to my LinkedIn account?

No account password is needed. We prepare recommendations and copy for your review; you remain in control of making changes to your profile.

Can you guarantee more profile views or job offers?

No. Results depend on your experience, market conditions, network, activity and LinkedIn's changing systems. We focus on clarity, relevance and professional positioning.

Will you invent achievements to make my profile stronger?

Never. We work with information you provide and may ask questions to clarify evidence. All claims should be accurate and approved by you.

Can you optimise my profile for a career change?

Yes. We can help identify transferable skills and frame your experience in language relevant to your intended direction.

Do you publish posts on my behalf?

Content strategy and draft suggestions can be scoped separately. Nothing is published without your review and explicit approval.
