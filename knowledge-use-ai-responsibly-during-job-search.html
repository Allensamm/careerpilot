How to Use AI Responsibly During Your Job Search

AI can help organise your job search, understand role requirements and practise interviews. Use it as support for your judgement, not a replacement for it.

Use AI as an assistant, not an authority

AI can summarise a job description, organise application tasks, suggest clearer wording and generate interview practice questions. It cannot verify your personal history or make hiring decisions. Treat its output as a draft that requires your review.

Tailor your CV without inventing experience

Ask AI to identify the main requirements in a vacancy and compare them with experience you provide. Use the comparison to find relevant evidence and transferable skills. Do not allow a tool to add qualifications, employers, responsibilities, dates, metrics or achievements that are not true.

Protect personal and confidential information

Avoid pasting identity documents, home addresses, private references, confidential employer material or sensitive personal details into AI services unless you have checked the provider's data practices and have a lawful reason to share them. Remove names and identifiers when they are not needed.

Keep your voice and professional judgement

AI-generated cover letters and LinkedIn copy can sound generic. Edit drafts so they reflect your real experience, natural voice and intended direction. Check spelling, names, role titles and every factual statement before sending.

Respect employer rules

Some employers restrict or specify permitted AI use in application tasks, assessments and interviews. Read the instructions and follow them. Do not use AI to misrepresent your independent work.

A practical, responsible workflow

Remove personal details from a vacancy, ask AI to list its key requirements, compare those requirements against your real experience, then manually verify every sentence in the final application.
