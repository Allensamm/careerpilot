Frequently asked questions

Job search support, clearly explained.

Can you guarantee that I will get a job?

No. Hiring decisions depend on employers, market conditions, qualifications and many factors outside our control. We focus on improving the quality, clarity and organisation of your search.

Do you apply for jobs on my behalf?

You remain in control of applications and submissions. We can help research opportunities and prepare materials, but we do not submit applications or make representations without your review and explicit approval.

Where do you find job opportunities?

We can research public employer career pages and accessible job boards based on your agreed criteria. Availability and completeness vary by source; we do not claim access to every posting across the internet.

Can you tailor my CV to a specific vacancy?

Yes. We can align your genuine experience with the requirements of a vacancy, without inventing qualifications, responsibilities or achievements.

Do you support career changers?

Yes. We can help identify transferable skills and explain how your experience relates to your target direction.
